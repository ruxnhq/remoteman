# Contributing

A change reaches `main` through a pull request. Say what changed and why, and how you tried it.

## Legal rules

These matter more than anything else here.

- Send only what you wrote yourself, or open source code under a licence that fits GPL-3.0-or-later. Keep its notice, say where it comes from, and add it to the Licence section of the [README](README.md).
- Nothing copied from closed software: no code, data, tables or text.
- Nothing leaked or confidential, and nothing an NDA or an agreement with your employer covers.
- No secrets, and no paths from your own machine.
- Everything here is GPL-3.0-or-later, and that includes what you add. The README's Licence section lists the parts that keep their own licences.
- If you aren't sure about something, open an issue and ask before you write it.

By sending a pull request you confirm that you have the right to give your work under this licence.
