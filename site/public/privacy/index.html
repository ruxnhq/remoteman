remoteman Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · RUXN LLC

remoteman lets you see and control your own computers from your other devices. It has no accounts and no servers of ours in between: each app talks directly to the computers you pair it with.

What we collect

Nothing. remoteman does not send us any personal data, usage data, analytics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising or third-party tracking code.

What stays on your devices

  • The list of computers you pair with, their addresses and their security fingerprints.
  • An identity key each device creates for itself, used to prove to your computers that it is the device you paired.
  • Your settings, such as picture size, sound and input preferences.

These stay on the device, in its own storage, and are removed when you delete the app.

What goes between your devices

While you are connected, the screen picture, sound, your keyboard, mouse, touch and pen input, and — only when you switch them on — clipboard, files, your device's microphone and its camera travel between your device and your computer. The connection is encrypted end to end (TLS 1.3 over QUIC) and goes directly between the two, on your local network or over a network you choose. None of it passes through or is kept by us.

Permissions

The apps ask for local network access, to find and reach your computers, and only when you switch on the matching feature for the microphone, the camera or Face ID. You can turn any of these off in your device's settings.

Children

remoteman is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
