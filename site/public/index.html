On every screen

The same computer, wherever you pick it up.

On a tablet on the sofa, a phone on the train or another computer at your desk: the same desktop, the same windows, just where you left them.

Features

Everything a computer does, wherever you are.

Not a picture of a screen: the whole computer, with what you hear, say, draw and play.

A picture worth sitting in front of

The computer encodes its desktop on its own graphics card, in AV1, HEVC or H.264, up to 10-bit HDR, at the refresh rate of the screen you watch on. Text stays sharp when nothing moves, and motion stays smooth when it does.

A display of its own

Your device becomes another display of the computer, its main display, or its only one, as you choose.

Sound, both ways

Hear the computer on your device, and talk through your device's microphone as the computer's own.

Your camera as its webcam

Calls on the computer see you through your phone, tablet or laptop camera, as a camera every app can pick.

Pen, touch and controllers

Draw with pressure, scroll with your fingers, and play with the game controller in your hands.

Clipboard and files

Copy on one, paste on the other, and send files across, when you switch it on.

Private by design

Just your devices. Nobody in the middle.

remoteman has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your device pairs with your computer once, and from then on they talk to each other directly, on your network or one you choose.

  • Encrypted end to end with TLS 1.3 over QUIC, each side proven by its own key.
  • Nothing about you is collected: no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking.
  • Your pairings and settings stay on your devices.
